My Top 5 Movies

  1. Our Planet (2019, Netflix series treated as film experience): Stunning cinematography of Earth’s ecosystems that reminds me why I chose Applied Biology.
  2. The Martian (2015): A botanist survives on Mars using science and potatoes — pure inspiration for problem-solving under pressure.
  3. Avatar (2009): Beautiful portrayal of interconnected ecosystems and the cost of environmental destruction.
  4. Hidden Figures (2016): Brilliant story of intelligence, perseverance and breaking barriers — motivates me in STEM.
  5. Life of Pi (2012): A powerful mix of survival, nature and storytelling that stays with you long after the credits.
