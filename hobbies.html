What I Do When I'm Not Studying

Nature Walks & Plant Spotting

I love walking through forests, botanical gardens and nearby reserves. Identifying trees, birds and insects keeps me connected to the living world I study. Best moment: finding a rare orchid near campus after weeks of searching.

Skill level: 8/10

Football

Weekend matches with friends are non-negotiable. I play midfield and enjoy the teamwork and strategy. It keeps me fit and clears my mind after long lab sessions.

Skill level: 7/10

Reading Science & Nature Books

From popular science to field guides, I always have a book open. Recent favourites include works on Kenyan biodiversity and stories of great field biologists.

Skill level: 9/10

Hobby of the Month

Sketching insects and leaves — I keep a small notebook and pencil for quick drawings during field trips. It helps me notice fine details I would otherwise miss.
